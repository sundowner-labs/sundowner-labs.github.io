Delete your Daystack account

Daystack AI, the day planner and health tracker by Sundowner Labs

You can delete your Daystack account and its data at any time.

In the app (straight away)

Go to Profile → Cloud Sync → Delete account and confirm.

Without the app (within 30 days)

  1. Email support@sundownerlabs.com.au from the email address you use to sign in to Daystack, with the subject "Delete my Daystack account".
  2. We'll confirm the request with you, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and its email address.
  • Every task, event and habit you shared to Homestack, and your Homestack connection key. Homestack removes its copy the next time it refreshes.

Everything else you entered in Daystack (tasks, health logs, notes, goals and settings) is stored only on your phone, never on our servers. Uninstall the app to remove it. Rate-limiting logs for the AI Fitness Assistant hold a device ID and IP address, not your account, and are deleted once they are more than two days old. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat; email us if you'd like RevenueCat's record removed. We keep no copy of deleted data beyond our hosting provider's short-term backup recovery window.

Deleting some of your Daystack data without deleting your account

  • Anything you've entered: delete it in the app. It's stored on your phone, so it's gone straight away.
  • Items shared to Homestack: turn off "Show on family calendar" on the item, or delete it. It's removed from our servers straight away, and from Homestack on its next refresh.
  • Your Homestack connection: sign out of Cloud Sync in Profile, or email us to remove your connection key.
  • Anything else: email support@sundownerlabs.com.au from your sign-in email address, saying what you'd like removed. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.
