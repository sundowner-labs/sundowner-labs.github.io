Daystack AI Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Daystack AI ("Daystack") is a personal day planner and health tracker for Android made by Sundowner Labs, an Australian business. This policy explains what information Daystack handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

The short version: Daystack keeps your data on your phone. Only a few optional features send anything off the phone, and only when you use them. There are no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and we never sell your information.

Information that stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored on your device: tasks, events, habits, goals, journal notes, weight, blood pressure, water, sleep, mood, meditation, workouts, medications and supplements, and your settings. Reminders are scheduled on the phone, and step counts are read on the phone. None of this is sent to us, except as described below.

Information that leaves your phone, only when you use the feature

Sign-in (optional)

If you sign in, we collect your email address. It is held by our sign-in provider, Supabase, in Daystack's own project. Sign-in is by an emailed link, so Daystack never stores a password for you.

Homestack sharing (optional)

If you mark a task, event or habit "Show on family calendar", that item is uploaded so it can appear on your family's Homestack calendar. Only items you mark this way are uploaded. Homestack, another Sundowner Labs app, reads them using a personal connection key that you create in Daystack, and keeps a copy so your family's calendar can show them. We store the key only in hashed form. Turning sharing off, or deleting the item, removes it from Daystack's servers straight away, and Homestack removes its copy the next time it refreshes (about once a minute while a Homestack device is open).

AI Fitness Assistant (optional)

The assistant asks for your agreement before it sends anything, and sends nothing if you don't agree. Once you agree, each message you send goes through Daystack's server to Anthropic, which writes the reply. With each message the app also sends your 20 most recent workouts and, if you've entered them in your profile, your weight, height, age and gender, so the replies fit you. Our server does not keep any of this. To prevent abuse, we log a random device ID and your IP address for each request; entries older than two days are deleted the next time anyone uses the assistant. Anthropic handles what you send under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by RevenueCat, which receives your purchase details. Payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card details.

Health information

Daystack lets you log health information such as blood pressure, weight and medications. It stays on your phone. The only health information that leaves your phone is what the AI Fitness Assistant sends, after you agree: your recent workouts and your profile's weight, height, age and gender. Daystack is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. These service providers handle the limited information described above, only to provide Daystack:

  • Supabase — sign-in and Homestack sharing.
  • Anthropic — the AI Fitness Assistant.
  • RevenueCat — subscriptions.
  • Google Play — app distribution and payments.

Items you share to Homestack are also held by Homestack. We may disclose information if required by Australian law.

Where information is stored

Daystack's servers (Supabase) are in Sydney, Australia, and so are Homestack's. Some information is processed outside Australia: Anthropic (the AI Fitness Assistant) and RevenueCat (subscriptions) may process it in the United States and other countries.

How long we keep information

  • Information on your phone stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Your sign-in email is kept until you delete your account.
  • Items shared to Homestack are deleted when you turn sharing off or delete the item.
  • Rate-limiting logs (device ID and IP address) are deleted once they are more than two days old, the next time anyone uses the assistant.

Deleting your information

You can delete any item in the app at any time, and turning Homestack sharing off deletes the shared items from our servers. To delete your account, go to Profile → Cloud Sync → Delete account in the app. This deletes your account, the items you shared to Homestack and your connection key straight away. You can also ask us to delete it without the app; see Delete your Daystack account. Uninstalling the app removes the information stored on your phone.

Security

Information sent from the app is encrypted in transit. Information on our servers is protected so that each account can reach only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

Age

Daystack is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe someone under 18 has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you by emailing privacy@sundownerlabs.com.au. If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to fix it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
