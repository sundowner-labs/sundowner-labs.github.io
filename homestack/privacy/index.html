Homestack Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Homestack is a shared family calendar made by Sundowner Labs, an Australian business. This policy explains what information Homestack collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

The short version: Homestack only collects what it needs to run your family's calendar. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell or share your information for marketing.

Information we collect

Your account

  • Adults and teenagers: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we can't see them. If you sign in with Google, Apple or Microsoft instead, we receive your name and email address from them and store no password.
  • Children's and calendar-only members: a household admin can add family members who don't have an email address. For these we store a name and, if the admin sets one, a PIN (stored in hashed form). Calendar-only members (for example a baby or a pet) can't sign in at all.
  • Optional profile details: a birthdate (used only to show a member's age to the household admin), a colour and an emoji picture.

What your household adds

  • Calendar events, tasks, rewards and points, shared lists, and a bin-night schedule.
  • Invitations, which include the email address of the person being invited. The invite code itself is stored only in hashed form.
  • Photos you choose as a background or screensaver picture. These are stored privately for your household and are only used for that.

Connected apps (optional)

If you connect your own Daystack account, you give Homestack a personal connection key created in Daystack. The key is stored securely on our servers and is never shown to other family members. Homestack uses it only to fetch the items you have chosen to share from Daystack, and displays them on your family's calendar. Information only flows from Daystack to Homestack, never the other way. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which deletes the key and the shared items from Homestack.

You can also link a Google, Outlook or iCloud calendar by its private calendar address. The address is stored on our servers and never sent back to any device; Homestack uses it only to fetch that calendar's events and show them on your family's calendar. Disconnecting deletes the address and the events it brought in.

Location

If you allow it, Homestack uses your device's approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) to show the local weather forecast. It's only used while the app is open, it's sent to our weather provider (Open-Meteo) to get the forecast, and Homestack does not store it. Weather is optional; the app works without location access.

Stored only on your device

Display preferences (layout, background, state for public holidays and similar settings) are kept on the device itself. To let family members switch who is using a shared tablet, the device also remembers which adult email addresses have signed in on it. Passwords are never stored on the device. Reminders for events, bin night and tasks are scheduled on the phone itself; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

How we use it

Only to provide Homestack's features: showing and syncing your household's calendar, tasks, rewards and lists across your family's devices, letting members sign in, and keeping accounts secure (for example, temporarily locking a PIN after repeated wrong attempts).

Who can see your information

  • Your household. Calendar events, tasks, lists, rewards and shared Daystack items are visible to the members of your household. Household admins can add, change and remove members.
  • Service providers who help us run Homestack, only as needed to provide it:
    • Supabase — our database, sign-in and file storage provider. Homestack's data is hosted in Sydney, Australia.
    • Google, Apple and Microsoft — only if you choose to sign in with them.
    • Open-Meteo — receives an approximate location to return a weather forecast.
    • Google Play — distributes the app.
  • We may disclose information if required by Australian law.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Children

You must be 13 or older to create your own Homestack account, and teenagers join a household only when invited by its admin. Homestack is designed to be set up and managed by adults for their family. Accounts for children under 13 can only be created by a household admin (a parent or guardian), and we collect as little as possible about them: a name, and optionally a PIN, a birthdate, a colour and a picture. If you believe a child's information has been added without a parent's or guardian's consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted so that each household can only see its own data, and sensitive values such as passwords, PINs and connection keys are never exposed to other users. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is active. A household admin can remove a member from the household in the app. You can delete your own account in the mobile app under Security → Delete account; it's deleted straight away. If you're your household's last admin, this also deletes the household, its photos and its children's accounts. Failed PIN attempts are recorded only to lock out repeated guessing.

To delete a family member's account or your whole household without the app, email us at privacy@sundownerlabs.com.au from the email address on the account, or see Delete your Homestack account. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. Removing the app from a device deletes the settings stored on that device.

Your rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you by emailing privacy@sundownerlabs.com.au. If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to fix it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
