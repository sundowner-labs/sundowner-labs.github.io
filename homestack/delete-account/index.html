Delete your Homestack account

Homestack, the family calendar app by Sundowner Labs

You can ask us to delete your Homestack account and its data at any time. You don't need the app installed to do this.

How to request deletion

  1. Email privacy@sundownerlabs.com.au from the email address you use to sign in to Homestack, with the subject "Delete my Homestack account".
  2. Tell us what you'd like deleted:
    • Just my account — you're removed from your household; the rest of your family keeps using it.
    • A family member's account (for example a child's PIN account or a calendar-only member) — household admins only.
    • My whole household — every member account you manage and everything in the household (household admins only).
  3. We'll confirm the request with you, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details: name, email address, password, PIN and any optional birthdate, colour or picture.
  • Things you added that belong only to you, such as a connected Daystack account and the items it shared.
  • If you delete a whole household: all of its calendar events, tasks, rewards and points history, lists, bin-night schedule, invitations and background photos.

If you leave a household that others still use, events and tasks you created for the family can stay so the family calendar isn't left with gaps; ask us if you'd like those removed too. We don't keep backups of deleted accounts beyond our hosting provider's short-term recovery window.

Deleting some of your Homestack data without deleting your account

You can keep your Homestack account and remove just some of your data.

In the Homestack app (deleted straight away)

  • Calendar events, tasks, lists and rewards: open the item and delete it. Household admins can delete any item; members with "edit their own items" can delete their own.
  • A family member and their profile: household admins go to + Task → Add family member and tap Remove from household.
  • Daystack items: go to Settings → Connected apps → Disconnect. Your connection key and every Daystack item shown in Homestack are deleted immediately.
  • Background photo: choose a new photo, or switch the background back to a colour, in Settings → Background.
  • Birthdate: clear it on the Family screen.

By email (within 30 days)

For anything else — for example your points history, invitations you sent, or a background photo you no longer have access to — email privacy@sundownerlabs.com.au from your sign-in email address, saying what you'd like removed. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What's kept

Your account itself (name, email address and sign-in details) is kept, because you're keeping your account. Data deleted in the app is removed immediately; we keep no copy beyond our hosting provider's short-term backup recovery window, after which it's gone.
